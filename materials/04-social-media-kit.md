## Launch sequence — six posts

Run these over roughly three weeks. Each has a suggested image from the site's `img/` folder.

### Post 1 — The name *(image: community-event.jpg)*

> In a village, the **gramotsav** is the day everyone gathers. To sing. To perform. To hand the stories down.
>
> It isn't entertainment. It's how a culture survives — and how a child first discovers they have something worth saying out loud.
>
> We took the name because we think that's also how children learn best.
>
> Gramotsav Foundation. Registered in Patna, March 2026.
> मिट्टी से मंच तक — from the soil to the stage.

### Post 2 — The method *(image: harmonium-hands.jpg)*

> Most music teaching starts with notation on a page.
>
> That works right up until the child in front of you cannot see the page.
>
> So we built the other way round. **Audio-first.** Listen. Demonstrate. Say it out loud. Repeat it. Feel the rhythm. Put your hands on the instrument.
>
> Not a workaround. A method — designed from the first session for the students who are usually designed around.

### Post 3 — The repertoire *(image: music-class-board.jpg)*

> We could teach anything. We teach Bhojpuri, Maithili and Magahi.
>
> Folk instruments. Festival songs. The stories that were carried by voice long before anyone wrote them down.
>
> Because a child learning their own tradition isn't just learning music. They're learning that where they come from is worth knowing.
>
> Culture isn't the decoration on the programme. It *is* the programme.

### Post 4 — The closure report *(image: harmonium-dholak.jpg)*

> Our first project has finished. So we published everything.
>
> **Learning Through Music** — an audio-first music programme for children and young people with visual impairment — is complete and formally closed. The full report is now on our website: the approach, the journey, the outcomes, the eight things we learned, and the parts that were hard.
>
> Most organisations publish what worked. We think you should be able to read the rest too.
>
> 🔗 Link in bio.

### Post 5 — What's next *(image: classroom-activity.jpg)*

> Music got us in the room. It isn't enough on its own.
>
> A student also needs accessible textbooks. A screen reader they can actually drive. Mobility skills. Someone to talk to about a career.
>
> The **Blind School Project** is the whole thing: accessible learning, assistive technology, life skills, music and arts, sports, and career exposure — inside a school that already exists, so the teachers there own it after we go.
>
> The concept note is public. We're looking for CSR and institutional partners.

### Post 6 — The ask *(image: guitar-community.jpg)*

> Five months old. One project designed, delivered and closed. Four governance policies in force. Registration and donor-deduction approval in hand.
>
> What we need now: partners.
>
> 🎸 **Instruments** — harmonium, dholak, tabla, keyboard, guitar
> 💻 **Devices** — screen-reader enabled computers, Braille displays
> 🤝 **CSR partnership** on the Blind School Project
> ⏱️ **Time** — music facilitators, special educators, career mentors in Patna
>
> gramotsavfoundation@gmail.com. We answer our own email.

## Ongoing content pillars

Rotate these so the feed doesn't become one long fundraising appeal.

1. **Session moments** — a photo from a real session, one honest sentence. No stock captions.
2. **One idea from the method** — explain a single teaching technique properly.
3. **The tradition** — a folk song, an instrument, a story, and why it's in our curriculum.
4. **Student work** — a performance clip or recording, with consent. Credit the young person as a musician.
5. **What we learned** — something that didn't work and what we changed.
6. **The ask** — a specific, concrete need. Never a vague "please donate".

**Suggested rhythm:** 2 posts a week. Pillars 1–5 four times for every one of pillar 6.

## Caption rules

- **Lead with the child's action, not their condition.** "He's been working on this rhythm for three weeks" beats "a visually impaired boy learns music".
- **One idea per post.** If it needs two, it's two posts.
- **No inspiration-porn framing.** Nobody is "an inspiration" for existing.
- **Never use a number we haven't counted.** See the numbers discipline note in `03-boilerplate-and-key-messages.md`.
- **Consent before posting** — parent or guardian *and* school, every time.
- **Credit the young person as a musician**, by first name only, and only with consent.
