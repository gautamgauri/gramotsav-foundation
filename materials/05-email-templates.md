>

>
> I'm approaching you because [**one specific, researched sentence — their stated CSR focus on education or disability, a Bihar programme they already run, something a colleague mentioned. If you cannot write this sentence honestly, do not send the email.**]
>
> We have completed and formally closed one project, *Learning Through Music* — an audio-first teaching method that does not depend on Braille notation. We published its full report, including what we would do differently: [link]
>
> We are now seeking a partner for the **Blind School Project** — accessible learning materials, assistive technology, life skills, arts, sports and career exposure, delivered inside an existing school so the teachers there own it afterwards. The concept note is here: [link]
>
> Would you be open to a 20-minute call? I can send a costed proposal for a specific site in advance if that is more useful.
>
> Warm regards,
> [Name] · [Role] · Gramotsav Foundation

>
> Thank you for writing — and for the offer.
>

>
> - **Music facilitators and practising artists** — running or co-running sessions
> - **Special educators and accessibility specialists** — improving our method
> - **Digital literacy and assistive-technology trainers**
> - **Career mentors** from any profession — a conversation with a working adult matters more than you'd think
> - **Documentation** — photography, video, writing
>
> Could you tell me which of these is closest to what you do, roughly how much time you have, and whether you're in Patna or remote? Then I can tell you honestly whether we can use you well right now, rather than saying yes and wasting your time.
>
> Warm regards,
> [Name]
