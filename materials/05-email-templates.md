# Email Templates

*Replace everything in `[square brackets]`. Delete any sentence you cannot personally stand behind.*

*These are written to sound like a person typed them, because a person will. Keep them plain. Do not smooth them out.*

## 1. Cold introduction to a CSR / corporate funder

**Subject:** `Inclusive education partnership in Patna — Gramotsav Foundation`

> Dear [Name],
>
> I'm writing from Gramotsav Foundation, a Section 8 non-profit registered in Patna in March 2026. We use music and folk culture as the medium for inclusive learning, mostly with children and young people who are blind or visually impaired.
>
> I'm approaching you because [**one specific, researched sentence: their stated CSR focus on education or disability, a Bihar programme they already run, something a colleague mentioned. If you cannot write that sentence straight, do not send the email.**]
>
> We have completed and formally closed one project, *Learning Through Music*. The teaching method is audio-first and does not depend on Braille notation. We published the full report, including what we would do differently: [link]
>
> We are now looking for a partner on the **Blind School Project**: accessible learning materials, assistive technology, life skills, arts, sports and career exposure, delivered inside an existing school so the teachers there own it afterwards. The concept note is here: [link]
>
> Would you be open to a 20-minute call? I can send a costed proposal for a specific site in advance if that is more useful.
>
> Warm regards,
> [Name] · [Role] · Gramotsav Foundation
> [sign-off block]

## 6. Replying to a volunteer enquiry

**Subject:** `Re: Volunteering with Gramotsav Foundation`

> Dear [Name],
>
> Thank you for writing — and for the offer.
>
> Before you commit: we are small and five months old, so volunteering here is hands-on and unglamorous rather than structured. What actually helps us:
>
> - **Music facilitators and practising artists** — running or co-running a baithak
> - **Special educators and accessibility specialists** — improving our method
> - **Digital literacy and assistive-technology trainers**
> - **Career mentors** from any profession — a conversation with a working adult matters more than you would think
> - **Documentation** — photography, video, writing
>
> Could you tell me which of these is closest to what you do, roughly how much time you have, and whether you're in Patna or remote? Then I can tell you straight whether we can use you well right now, instead of saying yes and wasting your time.
>
> Warm regards,
> [Name]
> [sign-off block]
